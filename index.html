Arches (Blue LED Light)

Science Fact: Arches are the most common fingerprint pattern, forming smooth, curved ridges. They represent simplicity and unity.

Quran Verse: "Indeed, We have created man in the best of stature." (Quran 95:4)

Explanation: Arches symbolize the simple and perfect design of Allah’s creation. Just as Allah created the human form in the best of stature, the arch represents the simplicity and balance found in the natural world.

Loops (Green LED Light)

Science Fact: Loops are the second most common pattern and have ridges that flow in one direction, making a loop-like shape. They are unique to individuals.

Quran Verse: "And We have certainly created man and We know what his soul whispers to him, and We are closer to him than [his] jugular vein." (Quran 50:16)

Explanation: Loops reflect the continuous flow of life and the interconnectedness of all things. The Quran verse emphasizes how Allah is closer to us than we can ever imagine, just as the loops connect in continuous patterns.

Whorls (Red LED Light)

Science Fact: Whorls are characterized by circular or spiral patterns and are less common than loops and arches. They are often seen as more complex.

Quran Verse: "He is the Creator of the heavens and the earth; He created all things in the best way." (Quran 32:7)

Explanation: Whorls represent complexity and intricacy. Just as Allah has created everything in the best possible way, the whorl design showcases the complexity and perfection inherent in His creation.

Ridges (Yellow LED Light)

Science Fact: Ridges are the foundation of fingerprint patterns, forming the unique lines that define each pattern. They enhance grip and tactile sensitivity, demonstrating a functional elegance in their design.

Quran Verse: "We have certainly created man in the best of stature" (Quran 95:4)

Explanation: Ridges, as the fundamental building blocks of fingerprints, illustrate the exquisite detail and purposefulness in Allah's creation. This verse highlights that humans are fashioned in the most excellent design, and ridges in our fingerprints are a testament to this perfection, aiding in our interaction with the world.
